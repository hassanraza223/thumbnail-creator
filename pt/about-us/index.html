About Us

Last updated: May 2026

ThumbnailCreator started as a simple idea: creators shouldn't need five different apps and an hour of their time just to download or design a thumbnail. We build free, fast tools that do one job well, so you can get back to making videos instead of fighting with software.

Our mission: Give creators fast, free tools for thumbnails — no login walls, no watermarks, no unnecessary steps.

Who We Are

We're a small team of web developers who got tired of watching creators spend hours manually downloading thumbnails, resizing images, and re-typing the same descriptions for every upload. So we built ThumbnailCreator to handle it in seconds instead — free, in your browser, with nothing to install.

What We Offer

Two free tools, with more on the way:

  • Baixador de miniaturas Paste any YouTube or Vimeo link and download the original thumbnail in HD or 4K — no watermark, no login.
  • Thumbnail Text & Design Tools Add bold, mobile-readable text and effects to your thumbnails, built for higher click-through rates on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.

Why It's Free

Every tool on this site is free to use, with no account required. We keep the lights on through select advertising and future premium features — but the core tools stay free for everyone, always.
